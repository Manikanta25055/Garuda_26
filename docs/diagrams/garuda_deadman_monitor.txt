The Deadman Monitor (Garuda_web.py _deadman_monitor, _send_tamper_email L1061) is a tamper-detection watchdog that assumes the absence of regular client heartbeats is evidence of physical interference with the device. Any authenticated browser session sends a periodic ping to POST /api/heartbeat, which updates the _last_heartbeat timestamp in the server. The _deadman_monitor daemon thread wakes every 10 seconds and computes the elapsed time since the last heartbeat; if the gap exceeds 180 seconds and the system is not in a scheduled maintenance window, the thread treats the condition as a tamper event. On a tamper event, two actions execute in parallel: trigger_software_alert fires the standard alert pipeline including a 3-second banner and WebSocket broadcast to any client that may still be connected, and _send_tamper_email (L1061) dispatches an SMTP email to the administrator address with the event timestamp and last-known system context. The 180-second window is intentionally generous to tolerate temporary network dropouts while still catching sustained disconnections that indicate the camera or Pi has been physically moved, covered, or powered down by an intruder.
