Can you help me test a home assistant for my project? It takes 2 minutes.

Imagine a room with a ceiling light and a fan. A camera in the room can tell:
- whether anyone is in the room, and how many people
- how long the room has been occupied, or empty
- where a person is: near the door, at the desk, or in the middle
- whether they are standing, sitting or walking
- how bright the room is

The system also knows the time of day, the temperature and humidity, and
whether the light and fan are on.

Write 3 to 5 instructions you would give this room so that it acts by itself
from now on. Each one should say WHEN something should happen and WHAT the
light or fan should do. Write them the way you would say them to a person:
simple or complicated, exact or vague, all fine. English, or Telugu/Hindi in
English letters, both fine.

If an instruction could be read two ways, add what you meant in brackets.

Please don't look at anyone else's answers first. Reply with one instruction
per line. Thank you!
