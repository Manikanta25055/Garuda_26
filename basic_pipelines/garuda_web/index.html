How Garuda works, page by page

Garuda Security System

Garuda is a home security and home automation console that runs entirely on a Raspberry Pi 5 with a Hailo-8L. Detection, recordings and the house rules stay on the Pi; only Narada's language model, its voice, alert e-mails and the optional remote-access tunnel use the internet.

What the app includes

  • Live camera with room sound, a snapshot button and 60-second clips.
  • Real-time detection and alerts, with e-mail and the Dynamic Island.
  • Captures: pictures, sound and video saved on their own when an alert starts, or on request.
  • Narada, a voice and text assistant that runs the house through its tools.
  • Devices, scenes, rules, shortcuts, schedules and an away mode.
  • An Events page with the timeline, alert history and saved captures, and Settings for everything else.

Home

Is the house OK right now: the security state, the last alert, whether the owner is home, the system modes, the live camera and the latest detections.

Camera card

The card shows the live camera. The frames per second the detector is processing are on System › System health.

The speaker button plays live sound from the USB microphone beside the video, with about 60 ms of delay. Your choice is remembered. Sound runs only while someone listens, so Narada keeps the microphone the rest of the time.

The video stream sends a new frame only when the browser has shown the last one, so a slow connection skips frames instead of falling behind.

Events

The Events tab holds what happened: the Alert Activity heatmap, the date-filtered Activity timeline and the Captures saved for each alert.

Settings

Settings opens Automations, House activity, Notifications, System (with System health, the console and the emergency stop), Logs, Narada phrases and this help.

Dynamic Island

The pill at the top shows live states such as alerts, all clear, recording, Narada activity and offline, so the system status stays visible on every page. Tap it to open it.

Detection and Alerting

YOLOv6n runs on the Hailo-8L and reads every camera frame in real time. Objects above the confidence threshold are checked against the alert and watch rules.

Detection controls

Danger LabelThe object that starts an alert.
Watch LabelsObjects that are logged quietly, without an alert.
Detection ThresholdModel sensitivity, from 0.05 to 0.95.

Modes

Privacy Blur hides faces, Night Mode raises alert readiness, DND keeps things quiet, Idle puts the system on standby, Email Alerts Off stops alert e-mails and Emergency overrides the quieter modes. A mode that silences alerts is only set when you clearly ask for it.

Captures

Garuda can keep pictures, sound and video from the camera, so you have evidence of what happened when an alert started.

What gets saved

SnapshotA still picture (JPEG) of the camera.
Sound1 to 30 seconds from the room microphone (WAV).
Video1 to 60 seconds of camera video (WebM) with the room sound mixed in. It is encoded while it records, so it is ready as soon as it ends.

Three ways to capture

  • Ask Narada by voice or text: "save a picture", "record 20 seconds of video", "email me the last captures".
  • Alert shortcuts: a shortcut can start on an alert (any, danger, night presence or camera covered) and run capture steps, for example a picture, the next 10 seconds of sound and a video of the same moment.
  • The API, for your own scripts (below).

Where to find them

Open Events and look at Captures. Each run is one card with players for its picture, sound and video. From there you can keep a capture, delete it or e-mail it to the alert recipients.

Storage and disk guard

Captures live on the Pi. When there are more than 500 of them or they pass 2 GB, the oldest go first. A capture marked keep is never removed this way.

Garuda always keeps 500 MB of the disk free. When space runs short it removes the oldest unkept captures, and if that is not enough it refuses the new capture instead of filling the disk. On start it also clears files the capture list does not know about.

Limits can be changed in .env with GARUDA_CAPTURES_MAX, GARUDA_CAPTURES_MAX_MB and GARUDA_CAPTURES_MIN_FREE_MB.

API

POST /api/captures/snapshotSave a picture now.
POST /api/captures/audioRecord sound for 1 to 30 s. Pass wait=false to record in the background.
POST /api/captures/videoRecord video for 1 to 60 s, also with wait=false.
GET /api/capturesList captures with a link to each file.
GET /api/captures/{id}/fileDownload one capture.
POST /api/captures/{id}/keepProtect a capture from clean-up.
DELETE /api/captures/{id}Delete a capture.
POST /api/captures/emailE-mail captures to the alert recipients.

Home and Automations

Garuda also runs the house: lights, fans, plugs and sensors, with rules that react to what the camera and the house see.

The Devices page lists scenes and devices by room. Type what you want in plain words, such as "fan off in 30 minutes" or "when I leave, turn everything off". Admins add devices with a name, room, MQTT topic and optional wattage.

MQTT broker

Devices talk to the broker over MQTT. Set DRISHTI_MQTT_USER and DRISHTI_MQTT_PASS to log in, and DRISHTI_MQTT_TLS=1 to encrypt the connection on port 8883 with the broker's certificate checked.

Narada Assistant

Narada is Garuda's assistant. Talk or type, and it reads the house and acts through its tools.

Voice or text, one page

Tap the microphone to talk. ElevenLabs listens, turns speech into text and speaks Narada's reply, hands-free until about 30 seconds of silence or a second tap. A long answer is spoken as its short version. Or type into the bar below the glyph. The glyph shows what Narada is doing: breathing when idle, following your voice while listening, orbiting while thinking and blooming while speaking.

One brain

Every request goes to NVIDIA NIM (Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, with Nemotron 3 Ultra as backup). Nothing changes without the model: if NIM is down, Narada says so and does nothing. Simple commands for several devices, and questions like "is the fan on?", take a faster lane and answer in about a second.

What Narada can do

  • Switch devices and scenes, set modes, and build rules, shortcuts and schedules.
  • Save and manage captures: picture, sound, video, list, keep, delete and e-mail.
  • Listen to the room (how loud it is) and play room sound in the chat.

Safety checks

Before a reply reaches you, Narada checks that what it says it did matches the real state of the house. Text read from house data is screened for hidden instructions; a suspicious string is treated as data only and you are told.

Keys

Keys live in .env on the Pi (NIM_API_KEY, ELEVENLABS_API_KEY). The browser only receives a one-conversation voice token.

Custom commands

Admins can teach Narada extra phrases and replies on the Custom Commands page.

Admin Controls

Admins configure alerts, detection, presence and access, and read the logs.

Email settings

Set the sender Gmail account, app password, recipients and alert cooldown. Send Test Email checks delivery from the app. Alert recipients also receive e-mailed captures.

System settings

Security health checks, detection threshold, danger label, privacy, known devices, the NVIDIA NIM key and model, watch labels and master keys.

Known devices

Owner presence comes from known phone MAC addresses. The app can scan the network and explains private or random MAC settings on phones.

Logs and exports

Logs are split into System, Detection, Presence and Voice. Admins can filter the current session, export it, or download the full archive.

Feedback

Feedback is stored on the server only. Anyone signed in can send it; admins read the inbox in the app.

Access and Security

Sign-in

1Credentials
→
2Code by e-mail

3Session

Users sign in with a username and password. Admins also enter a one-time code sent by e-mail. Master keys give instant admin access and unlock the logs.

Sessions

Sessions last 1 hour for admins and 5 days for users with "Remember me". Signing out ends the session on the server too.

Safeguards

  • Rate limits on sensitive routes, and the same answer for known and unknown names.
  • Admin-only endpoints for settings, logs and devices.
  • Phone MAC addresses and e-mail addresses are hidden from non-admins.
