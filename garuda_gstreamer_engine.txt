The GStreamer TAPPAS Pipeline Engine (Garuda_web.py L1368–1512) is the video ingestion and inference core of Project Garuda, implemented in the GStreamerDetectionApp class which extends the base GStreamerApp from hailo_rpi_common.py. The pipeline accepts three video source types — v4l2src for USB cameras, libcamerasrc for the Raspberry Pi CSI-2 camera, and filesrc for recorded video — and feeds frames through videoscale and videoconvert elements for format normalisation. From the preprocessor, the pipeline forks into two parallel branches: an inference branch that routes frames through HailoNet (the GStreamer element that dispatches to the Hailo-8L NPU over PCIe) followed by a HailoFilter element that runs NMS post-processing on the raw network output, and a bypass queue branch that carries time-aligned raw frames. Both branches converge at the HailoMuxer, which synchronises the annotated inference results with the corresponding raw frame before passing the merged buffer to an identity element where the app_callback probe (L1209) is attached. After the callback, a HailoOverlay element renders bounding boxes and class labels directly onto the frame, and the final frame is delivered to either a display sink or video sink depending on the runtime configuration.
